    How to read this
    • A person sits under whoever first brought them in. Later re-adds, removals and exits are listed in their history.
    • “In the group before the log” holds people who were already members when the export's history starts. The export doesn't say who added them.
    • WhatsApp shows saved contacts by name and everyone else by number, so one person may appear under both. This page matches exact text only.
    • WhatsApp sometimes writes “Someone added X” right after a named add or invite-link join of the same person. Those echoes are dropped. One add with no named adder remains.
    • A ~Name chip next to a number is the WhatsApp name saved in the phone's contacts list. Numbers without a chip aren't in the contacts.
    • “Chatted, but WhatsApp never recorded who added them” holds people who appear only as message senders. Invite-link join notifications can be switched off in WhatsApp, and then the export has no trace of how they got in.
